# Campus Customs: Storefront Design

**Goal:** make Bulldog Blue feel like a real, premium Campus Customs store, with a posh,
preppy New England look in Yale navy (think J.Crew or Banana Republic), not a generic
merch page.

## 1. Fonts

**Changed**
- Serif headlines, the wordmark and prices in *Cormorant Garamond*.
- Body text in clean *Jost*.
- Small uppercase, letter-spaced labels and navigation.
- A centered **BULLDOG BLUE** wordmark.

**Why it helps:** it reads as a heritage brand at a glance, so $58–$98 feels earned, not
pricey.

## 2. Color and hierarchy

**Changed**
- Navy is the **only** action color (buttons, header, footer).
- Ivory and cream backgrounds, with camel and brass accents.
- Red and amber appear **only** for stock warnings.
- A navy-and-ivory sailor stripe, square corners and hairline borders.

**Why it helps:** the next step (shop, ask, view) is always obvious, and "Sold out" or
"Limited sizes" can't be missed.

## 3. Motion

**Changed**
- **Hero:** five scenes (the Yale lawn, Harkness Tower, the sailboat, autumn brick, the
  harbor), each with its own camera move, crossfading every 4.6 seconds.
- **Scroll reveals:** sections ease in and product rows cascade.
- **Parallax** on the big photos.
- **Header:** slims and hides while scrolling down, and returns on scroll up.
- **Announcement bar:** rotates between its messages.
- **Reduced motion:** turned off for shoppers who ask their device for less motion.

**Why it helps:** the store feels alive, like a fashion brand, so shoppers keep scrolling,
and the hidden header gives products the whole screen.

## 4. Product presentation

**Changed**
- **"Worn by Bulldogs" lookbook:** five real models wearing our real pieces (real
  Unsplash photos with each product's actual print added and credited). It's a sideways
  row you can swipe or drag, and each look links to the item.
- **Lifestyle scenery:** real Yale and New England photos, paired with real products.
- **Product cards:**
  - color swatches, the sizes in stock, and "Sold out" / "Limited sizes" badges;
  - on hover, a **crossfade to the model wearing it**, plus a **quick view of sizes**.
- **Products page:** category tabs and Sort by. **Home:** shop-by-category tiles.
- **Product page:** a breadcrumb, colorway swatches, a size picker, a trust list (licensed,
  about 5–8 days to make, 30-day returns), and **"Ask our stylist about this piece"**.

**Why it helps:** shoppers see the clothes on a person and can tell "is my size in?"
before clicking. Tabs and sorting find the item in one or two clicks, and the trust list
removes last-minute doubts.

## 5. Chat and feel

**Changed**
- An **"Ask our stylist"** launcher with a "CC" monogram.
- "Answers from live inventory" with an online dot.
- A **"Now viewing: [item]"** bar on product pages.
- One-tap **suggested questions** and "typing" dots with live status.
- A brand-voice greeting ("Welcome aboard…") with no emojis.

**Why it helps:** it feels like a personal shopper, not a bot. Suggested questions remove
"what do I ask?", and "live inventory" plus "Now viewing" make the answers trustworthy,
so undecided shoppers buy.

*Kept from earlier problems:*
- products fit on screen without scrolling;
- white-background product photos;
- every card opens its product page;
- streaming chat;
- works down to phone width.
